💡 Pro Recording Tips

  • Keep the browser maximized in 16:9 PC widescreen (fits YouTube/Loom perfectly with zero black bars).
  • Wait ~1 second after Alexa finishes speaking before beginning your next spoken line.
  • Use Win + Alt + R (Xbox Game Bar) or Win + Shift + S (Snipping Tool Video) with microphone toggled ON.
