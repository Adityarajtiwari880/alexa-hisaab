### 💡 Quick Recording Tips
- **Aspect Ratio:** Keep browser maximized in 16:9 PC widescreen.
- **Audio Pause:** Wait ~1 second after Alexa finishes speaking before beginning your next sentence.
- **Hotkeys:** Use `Win + Alt + R` (Xbox Game Bar) or `Win + Shift + S` (Snipping Tool Video) with microphone toggled **ON**.
